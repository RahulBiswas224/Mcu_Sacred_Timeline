# Contributing to the Sacred Timeline

All variant timelines must be reviewed by the Time Variance Authority before
merging into `main`. Please follow these guidelines:

1. **One Nexus Event per branch.** Keep divergences focused and well-documented
   in `events/`.
2. **No unauthorized pruning.** Don't force-push over `main` — that's how you
   end up as a TVA case file.
3. **Character continuity.** If your branch adds or removes a character file,
   explain why in the commit message (see the Infinity War / Endgame commits
   for the pattern).
4. **Tag Phase-ending commits.** Every saga-concluding film gets a `phase-N` tag.

Variants who fail to comply will be reset to zero.
