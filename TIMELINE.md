# Nexus Events Log

A quick index of every branch/merge in this repo and what it represents:

- **thanos-2014-heist** — spawned when the 2014 Thanos learns of his future
  defeat during the Avengers' time heist; pruned (merged back) after Endgame.
- **loki-tva-timeline** — spawned when a 2012 Loki variant escapes with the
  Tesseract; this is the seed of the TVA storyline and the multiverse's discovery.
- **earth-838** — the alternate universe explored in *Doctor Strange in the
  Multiverse of Madness*, merged back into `main` once Strange returns home.

See individual files under `events/` for full write-ups.
