# Black Widow

**Phase:** Phase 3 (prequel)
**Director:** Cate Shortland
**Release Date:** 2021-07-09
**Box Office (worldwide, approx.):** ~$379M

## Cast
- Scarlett Johansson
- Florence Pugh
- David Harbour

## Synopsis
Set in the aftermath of Civil War, Natasha Romanoff confronts her past as a Russian spy and reunites with her surrogate family to take down the Red Room.
