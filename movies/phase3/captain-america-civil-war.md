# Captain America: Civil War

**Phase:** Phase 3
**Director:** Anthony & Joe Russo
**Release Date:** 2016-05-06
**Box Office (worldwide, approx.):** ~$1.15B

## Cast
- Chris Evans
- Robert Downey Jr.
- Scarlett Johansson
- Chadwick Boseman
- Tom Holland

## Synopsis
The Avengers fracture into opposing factions over a government act to regulate superheroes, introducing Black Panther and a new Spider-Man.
