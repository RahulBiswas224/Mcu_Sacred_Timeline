# Avengers: Endgame

**Phase:** Phase 3
**Director:** Anthony & Joe Russo
**Release Date:** 2019-04-26
**Box Office (worldwide, approx.):** ~$2.80B

## Cast
- Robert Downey Jr.
- Chris Evans
- Chris Hemsworth
- Scarlett Johansson
- Mark Ruffalo
- Jeremy Renner

## Synopsis
The surviving Avengers undertake a time heist across the Infinity Saga to undo Thanos's snap, leading to a final, universe-spanning battle.
