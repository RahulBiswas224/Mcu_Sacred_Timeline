# Iron Man 2

**Phase:** Phase 1
**Director:** Jon Favreau
**Release Date:** 2010-05-07
**Box Office (worldwide, approx.):** ~$624M

## Cast
- Robert Downey Jr.
- Scarlett Johansson
- Mickey Rourke
- Sam Rockwell

## Synopsis
Tony Stark battles palladium poisoning and a vengeful rival engineer while S.H.I.E.L.D. moves to secure his technology; Natasha Romanoff is introduced.
