# Iron Man

**Phase:** Phase 1
**Director:** Jon Favreau
**Release Date:** 2008-05-02
**Box Office (worldwide, approx.):** ~$585M

## Cast
- Robert Downey Jr.
- Gwyneth Paltrow
- Jeff Bridges
- Terrence Howard

## Synopsis
Billionaire engineer Tony Stark builds a powered suit of armor after being held captive overseas, becoming Iron Man and kicking off the modern superhero era.
