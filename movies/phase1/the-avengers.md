# The Avengers

**Phase:** Phase 1
**Director:** Joss Whedon
**Release Date:** 2012-05-04
**Box Office (worldwide, approx.):** ~$1.52B

## Cast
- Robert Downey Jr.
- Chris Evans
- Chris Hemsworth
- Scarlett Johansson
- Jeremy Renner
- Mark Ruffalo

## Synopsis
Loki leads an alien Chitauri invasion of Earth, forcing S.H.I.E.L.D. to assemble a team of superheroes to stop him and recover the Tesseract.
