# Ant-Man

**Phase:** Phase 2
**Director:** Peyton Reed
**Release Date:** 2015-07-17
**Box Office (worldwide, approx.):** ~$519M

## Cast
- Paul Rudd
- Evangeline Lilly

## Synopsis
Ex-con Scott Lang dons a suit that lets him shrink in scale, training under Hank Pym to pull off a high-stakes heist.
