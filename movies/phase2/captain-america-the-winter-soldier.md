# Captain America: The Winter Soldier

**Phase:** Phase 2
**Director:** Anthony & Joe Russo
**Release Date:** 2014-04-04
**Box Office (worldwide, approx.):** ~$714M

## Cast
- Chris Evans
- Scarlett Johansson

## Synopsis
Steve Rogers uncovers a HYDRA conspiracy hiding inside S.H.I.E.L.D. while facing a mysterious assassin from his past.
